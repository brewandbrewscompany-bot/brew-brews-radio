Data Deletion Instructions

Updated August 30, 2026

Louisburg Local primarily processes public business and community information. It does not require residents or local businesses to sign in with Facebook or Instagram in order to use the public feed.

Information submitted directly to Louisburg Local

If you submitted a factual correction (“Sherlock Note”) or another direct submission and want that submitted information removed, send a deletion request through the current Louisburg Local project contact channel and identify the item or submission clearly enough for it to be located. Do not send passwords, account tokens or other sensitive credentials.

Reactions and technical anti-abuse records

Louisburg Local may keep pseudonymous keys used to prevent duplicate reactions or duplicate correction submissions. These records are automatically purged under the project's retention rules. A deletion request may also ask for directly associated submission records to be removed when they can be identified.

Public information originating elsewhere

If the information originated on a business website, Facebook, Instagram, a school site, a government calendar or another external public source, Louisburg Local can remove its own feed/reference record after verification when appropriate. Removal from Louisburg Local does not delete the original information from the source platform. Requests to delete the original content must be directed to the source publisher or platform.

Meta integration data

Louisburg Local's planned Meta integration reads permitted public source content for aggregation. Access tokens for the technical integration are stored server-side and are not end-user profile records. Louisburg Local does not use Brew & Brews credentials or Meta assets to power this integration.
