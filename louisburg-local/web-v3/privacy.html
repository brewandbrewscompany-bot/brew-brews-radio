Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

Louisburg Local is a community aggregation service focused on public activity in Louisburg, Kansas. It organizes information that businesses, schools, public agencies, community organizations and other local sources already publish publicly on their websites, calendars and supported social-media channels.

Public source information

Louisburg Local may process public business or organization names, public post or event text, dates, times, public source URLs and public source media when that material is made available through an authorized or publicly accessible source. Louisburg Local links back to the original content whenever a content-level source is available.

Facebook and Instagram

Louisburg Local does not require local businesses to give Louisburg Local their Facebook or Instagram passwords, cookies or account credentials. When Meta APIs are used, access is limited to permissions granted to a separate Louisburg Local technical integration and to public content Meta permits that integration to read. Brew & Brews accounts and Meta assets are not used to authenticate Louisburg Local.

Information submitted on Louisburg Local

The site may accept lightweight reactions and factual correction reports (“Sherlock Notes”). Technical identifiers used to prevent duplicate submissions are pseudonymous and are retained only as needed for abuse prevention, verification and service integrity. Louisburg Local is not designed to collect sensitive personal information.

Visits and service operation

Louisburg Local may count site visits and store limited technical information needed to operate the feed, prevent duplicate actions, investigate errors and protect the service. The project does not sell personal information to advertisers.

Verification and publishing

Social and discovered activity is reviewed through Louisburg Local's verification pipeline before it becomes eligible for the public feed. Public-source collection does not mean every discovered item will be published.

Retention

Expired feed activity is removed from the current feed according to lifecycle rules. Pseudonymous anti-abuse keys and verification records are retained only for operational and audit purposes and are periodically purged according to project retention settings.

Third-party services

Links may open third-party websites such as Facebook, Instagram, local business sites, school systems or public calendars. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Data deletion

Instructions for requesting deletion of information submitted directly to Louisburg Local are available on the Data Deletion page.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Louisburg Local adds or changes source integrations. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date on this page.
