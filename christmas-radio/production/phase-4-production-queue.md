## What is actually left to FINISH

Writing is no longer the main bottleneck. The real remaining production work is:

1. Generate/export the chosen vocal and instrumental renders without wasting downloads on automatic A/B pairs.
2. Run the Christmas Radio Audio Audit on every real file: beginning, middle, late section, outro, lyric accuracy, pronunciation, artifacts, vocal behavior, energy, and transitions.
3. Promote only approved audio to canonical filenames.
4. Upload the approved binaries to `christmas-radio/audio/` when a binary-capable path is available.
5. Populate `christmas-radio/data/tracks.json` only after those binaries physically exist and pass audit.
6. Build the final station rotations from the real audited intros/outros rather than from genre labels alone.

## Current creative identity locked from the working session

- The world is **Louisburg at Christmas**.
- Brew & Brews should recur naturally by name, as a real local roastery and part of town life, not as an advertisement in every chorus.
- Show what people are doing: driving Amity, Broadway lights, snow days, wrapping gifts, family arriving, roastery work, bags going under trees, porch lights, kids, reindeer stories, Christmas morning, late-night quiet.
- Change genres aggressively enough to keep the dial interesting: crooner, roots rock, swing, rockabilly, folk, soul, orchestral, blues, old-country, kid/family, jazz, twisted opera, antique mono record, and instrumental resets.
- Keep every individual track musically coherent; do not cram every genre idea into one song.
- Christmas should be recognizable in the actual music, not only in the lyrics.
- Keep kid-friendly tracks fun for adults too: no chipmunk voices, nursery-rhyme production, laugh tracks, or cartoon overload.
- No fake crowds, unexplained applause, constant announcer chatter, or café framing.
