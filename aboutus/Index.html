Our Story

We roast coffee. We don't just put our name on a bag.

Brew & Brews started with a love for good coffee and a belief that it didn't need to be overcomplicated. We wanted coffee that tasted great, felt familiar, and still had enough character to make you stop after that first sip.

So we built our roasting operation around doing the important part ourselves. We source green coffee, roast it in small batches, develop each profile carefully, and package it for the people and businesses we serve.

Some coffees are bright and smooth. Some lean rich, nutty, or chocolatey. Some are dark, bold, and built for people who want a heavier cup. We don't force every coffee into the same mold.

Different coffees. Different roast profiles. One goal: make a cup you'll want to brew again tomorrow.
From Green Coffee to Finished Roast

The roasting is where the personality shows up.

Heat, timing, airflow, development, and experience all shape the final cup. That's the part we obsess over.

What Happens Here

From raw coffee to the bag in your hand.

STEP 01

Source

We start with green coffees selected for the flavor, body, sweetness, and roast style we're after.

STEP 02

Roast

Each coffee gets a roast profile designed to bring out what we actually want in the cup.

STEP 03

Rest + Pack

STEP 04

Brew

Then it heads to homes, businesses, wholesale customers, and retail shelves ready for the part that matters most: drinking it.
